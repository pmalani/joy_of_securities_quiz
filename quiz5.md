## Moby's Sardines

1. Ahab will buy a can of sardines for $100 from Dick a year now (a future)
1. The (forward) price of the can will be higher (contango) or lower (backwardation) than current (spot) price? Higher because (cost of carry)
    1. Need to borrow monry
    1. Store in a safe place 
    1. Transportation
1. Lower prices (backwardation) is rare
    1. E.g. Oil futures at -$35
1. How will the future be settled a year from now?
    1. Physical: Ahab get the can; Dick get $100
    1. Cash: Just exchange the difference between spot price year from now, say $110, and contract price of $100. Ahab gets $10. Dick keep the can. (Is there a can?)
        1. Common in currency forward, known as non-deliverable forward (NDF)

## What kind of derivative am I? Perps a.k.a Perpetual Futures
1. The current price is $100
1. The future price can be
    1. More
        1. Positive funding rate
        1. Longs pay shorts
    1. Less
        1. Negative funding rate
        1. Shorts pay longs
1. How often?
    1. Every few hours (e.g. 8 hours)
1. How much?
    1. Interest (e.g. 0.02%) a.k.a Funding Rate
    1. 0.02% every 8 hours is 24.48% annually
1. Use leverage (e.g. 10x)
1. No expiry date
    1. Can close out position anytime
1. Similarities with other securities like
    1. Swap with the exchange of funding rate
    1. Repo (to buy back at a future date) or Reverse Repo
